## About Me

I am an Electronic Systems Product Engineer on the OBD Infrastructure Team at [Cummins Inc.](https://www.cummins.com/), working on diagnostic validation for the Barracuda/CSAR program. I recently completed my M.S. in Engineering (Electrical Engineering concentration) at Texas A&M University–Corpus Christi, advised by Dr. Ruby Mehrubeoglu. I received my B.S. in Telecommunication Engineering from BUITEMS, Pakistan, in 2018.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on AI-driven anomaly and defect detection deployed on edge and embedded hardware for reliability-critical systems, applied to photovoltaic/renewable energy infrastructure, environmental monitoring, and vehicle diagnostics. My M.S. thesis developed a deep learning-based classification and segmentation pipeline for photovoltaic panel surface defects, deployed on a Raspberry Pi 5 with real-time IoT monitoring.
